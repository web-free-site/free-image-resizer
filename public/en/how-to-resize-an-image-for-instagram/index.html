How to resize an image for Instagram

Instagram crops photos that do not match the frame you chose (feed post, story, or profile photo). Resizing and cropping first gives you control over what stays in the picture. Free Image Resizer can do this in the browser.

Steps

  1. Check the current frame you need. Instagram's available sizes can change. In the Instagram app, start a post or story and note whether you need a square feed image, a portrait post, or a full-screen story, then resize to that shape.
  2. Open Free Image Resizer. On the English site, open the resize tool and add the photo from your device. The file is not uploaded to our servers.
  3. Set the shape, then the size. Crop to the aspect ratio you need first, then reduce the pixel size if the file is very large. Avoid stretching a small image up to a larger size; that only adds blur.
  4. Export and review on a phone. Download the result and open it on a phone in both portrait and landscape if you can. Then upload it in Instagram and confirm the crop is what you expected.

Stories versus feed posts

Stories and reels usually fill a tall phone screen. Feed posts are often square or slightly taller than wide. Use a separate crop for each format instead of forcing one file into both.

Quality

If the picture looks soft, go back to the original file and export again at a larger size, then let Instagram do the last bit of compression.
